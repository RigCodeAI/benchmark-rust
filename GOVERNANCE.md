# Governance

ZeroSurface currently maintains BenchmarkRust. Maintainers own repository
integrity, release provenance, case review, and contributor conduct; they may not
alter truth merely because a ZeroSurface product disagrees with it.

Truth-changing pull requests require review from someone other than the author of
the corresponding scanner implementation. Product regressions must be fixed in the
product unless independent evidence demonstrates that benchmark truth is wrong.

Scanner vendors may submit reproducible raw results and integration adapters. As
independent adoption grows, maintainers will invite outside reviewers and may move
truth adjudication to a neutral multi-organization group.
