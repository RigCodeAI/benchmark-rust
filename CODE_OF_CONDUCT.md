# Community conduct

We expect participants to be respectful, specific, evidence-driven, and welcoming.
Disagreement about security truth is normal; personal attacks, harassment,
discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and
may restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to
the maintainers. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed when adjudicating truth.
